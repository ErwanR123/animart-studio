# Museum Companion — Product Vision

## Product thesis
Museum Companion is not merely an AI audioguide and not a database of scanned paintings. It is a cultural exploration companion that helps people connect what they encounter in the real world to a progressively richer understanding of art, places and human history.

A photograph of an artwork can start a discovery: identify it, explain what matters, provide personalized audio or an immersive scene, answer questions, and connect the work to people, events, movements and places. The discovery then becomes part of the user's Explorer Notebook.

## Core promise
**Every real-world cultural discovery becomes a piece of a larger story.**

The user should gradually move from “I saw this painting” to “I understand where this belongs and how it connects to things I have already seen.”

## Core loop
Discover → Visit → See real artwork → Learn / experience → Collect → Connect to history → Play / reinforce → Unlock context → Explore again.

## Product principles
- The real museum experience comes first. The phone should enrich reality, not replace it.
- Personalization should make explanations and routes more relevant, not create noise.
- Collection mechanics support understanding; they are not the purpose of the product.
- The user's notebook is personal, while historical truth and cultural context remain shared.
- Mini-games reinforce discoveries the user actually made whenever possible.
- Audio is important during physical visits because users should spend more time looking at art than at their phone.
- The long-term scope can extend from paintings to sculpture, artifacts, architecture, monuments and historical places.

## Long-term vision
A visual representation of the culture the user has encountered in the real world — with museums as the strongest starting environment.

## Anti-goals
Do not drift into:
- a generic AI chatbot for art;
- Pokémon Go for paintings;
- Duolingo for art history;
- an infinite social/content feed;
- a replacement for museums or human attention;
- gimmicks added only because they are technically possible.
