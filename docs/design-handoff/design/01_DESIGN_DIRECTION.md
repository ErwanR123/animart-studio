# Museum Companion — Art Direction

## Direction
**Contemporary cultural explorer's notebook.**

The product combines a modern mobile interface with editorial art-book sensibility, a hand-built explorer notebook, subtle Italian artistic influence and a recurring illustrated Guide.

The intended tension is:

**Modern interface + illustrated soul.**

The main app should remain clean, calm and highly usable. The Notebook, discoveries, chapter reveals and mini-games can become more expressive.

## Visual keywords
Warm, cultured, curious, editorial, tactile, handcrafted, Mediterranean/Italian, museum-quality, exploratory, human.

## Visual layers
### Main application
Modern, restrained, editorial. Real artwork and museum photography dominate.

### Explorer Notebook
More tactile and expressive: sketches, annotations, stamps, historical maps, collage and connecting lines. Still contemporary; never pirate parchment.

### Mini-games
Manipulating cultural material inside the same visual world: artwork fragments, paper cards, timelines, pins, connections and stamps. Never arcade styling.

## Explicitly avoid
- glassmorphism;
- neon / purple AI gradients;
- generic SaaS dashboards;
- excessive shadows;
- cartoon UI everywhere;
- childish educational-app styling;
- fake parchment on every screen;
- emoji as primary illustration;
- glossy 3D mascots;
- generic Pixar/Disney look;
- over-detailed, over-lit “AI generated” concept-art aesthetics.

## Real art vs illustration
Original artworks remain visually authentic. Do not redraw famous artworks in the Guide's style when the original can be shown. The contrast between real art and the illustrated Guide is part of the identity.
